# Support Atelier TryOn / 支持项目

## Sponsor / 赞助

[Support on Afdian / 通过爱发电赞助](https://afdian.com/a/jerry)

Afdian is the project's only financial support link. Voluntary contributions help with ongoing development, GPU testing, and documentation. They do not purchase model licenses, inference access, or guaranteed support, and do not change any third-party license restrictions.

爱发电是本项目唯一的资金赞助入口。赞助完全自愿，用于支持持续开发、GPU 测试和文档维护，不代表购买模型授权、推理服务或保证响应的技术支持，也不改变第三方许可限制。

## Other ways to help / 其他助力方式

- Star the repository and share reproducible benchmarks. / Star 仓库，分享可复现的测试结果。
- Report bugs with sanitized logs and reproduction steps. / 提交脱敏日志和问题复现步骤。
- Contribute focused pull requests for tests, deployment, UI, or documentation. / 贡献测试、部署、UI 或文档改进。
- Share quality reports using authorized samples only. / 使用已授权素材提供质量报告。

Do not post payment receipts, identity documents, contact details, credentials, or private media in public issues. Supporter acknowledgments should be opt-in; do not publish names or amounts without consent. We do not publish an unverified donation total.

请勿在公开 Issue 中上传付款凭证、身份证件、联系方式、密钥或私人媒体。公开致谢应征得本人同意，未经许可不要公开姓名或金额；本项目不展示未经核实的捐款统计。
